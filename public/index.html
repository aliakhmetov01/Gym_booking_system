Week 6 Decision • Automatic Data Backup

Disaster Recovery & Ransomware Protection

Defends the core booking database against ransomware, physical loss, and data corruption.

Our Team Decision: Automatic Data Backup

If the database is damaged or encrypted by malware, the gym loses the schedule and bookings. A recent backup snapshot allows instant recovery and operational continuity.

Why Firewall was Postponed

Basic router settings, strong authentication, and careful staff permissions protect the network initially, while catastrophic permanent loss of the database would shut down the entire gym.
